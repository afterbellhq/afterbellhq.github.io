Privacy policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

Who we are

Afterbell is a science channel on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. This policy covers this website, our channels and the posting app we use to publish our own videos. Questions go to afterbellhq@gmail.com.

This website

This site sets no cookies and has no analytics, ads, trackers or content from other sites. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to run and protect the service, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Watching our videos

When you watch, like, comment on or share a video on YouTube, Instagram or TikTok, that platform handles your data under its own privacy policy. We see only what each platform shows every creator: totals such as views, likes and watch time, and the comments people post publicly. We do not collect, buy or sell personal data.

The Afterbell posting app

Afterbell runs a small private app on its own computer that publishes our videos to our own accounts and reads the statistics of those videos. It signs in only to the Afterbell accounts and is not offered to anyone else, so it never handles other people's accounts.

On YouTube the app uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the Afterbell channel, check their privacy status and read the channel's YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time and similar totals). On Instagram it uses the Instagram API to publish Reels to the Afterbell account and read their insights. On TikTok it uses the Content Posting API to send videos to the Afterbell account as drafts.

The app keeps its sign-in tokens and these statistics on our own computer. They are not shared, sold or used for advertising.

YouTube and Google

Because the app uses YouTube API Services, the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy also apply. Afterbell's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access given to the app can be removed at any time on the Google security settings page.

Children

Our videos are made for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes we will post the new version here with a new date.
